About each demo
  • Anisotropic Kuwahara

    Style-preserving image abstraction filter, studied after Kyprianidis et al. 2009. Structure tensor built from Sobel gradients; analytic eigen-decomposition orients 8 anisotropy sectors per pixel; polynomial-Gaussian weights (no exp()) and variance-weighted blending.

    DemoVideoPaper
  • Volumetric clouds

    Technique study — six-pass GLSL volumetric sky, after the real-time rendering course material linked below. Spherical-shell ray march with geometric step growth, 3-octave Perlin fBm vs tileable Worley detail with LOD split + proxy pre-test, Nubis + Beer-Lambert attenuation with powder term, layered Rayleigh/Mie/ozone atmosphere, 64² blue-noise + IGN dithering, auto-exposure, god rays, TAA + CAS composite. Time-of-day is a stylized sun-path parameter.

    DemoVideoPaper
  • Gaussian Surface Splatting 2D

    EM-fitted 76-component Gaussian mixture on a sphere; Tikhonov-regularized covariances decomposed to oriented ellipsoids; backface-culled and projected to screen-space conics; per-8×8-tile depth queue with max 8 splats; EWA-weighted accumulation into an Oklab GBuffer; analytic lights with ACES tonemapping.

    DemoVideoRelated paper (3DGS)
  • Gas particle clustering (port/study)

    Verified port/study of michael0884's original (mscfW7/ddcfzS). Double-buffered 4-pass GLSL pipeline; two clusters per texel bit-packed (masses, quantized positions, snorm velocities); mass-conserving merges; over-seeded splitting; 5×5 neighbor stencil with exp(-d²) repulsion; SDF-box boundary; capacity set by texel packing, not a fixed particle count.

    DemoVideoOriginals
  • Jump flooding SDF + SPH water

    1024² grid, 16×16 workgroups. 18 separable jump-flood passes with two-candidates-per-texel packing (branchless select-merge). 2D SPH water with 3 substeps, cubic-spline kernels, Tait pressure, Monaghan viscosity; particles quad-packed per cell with centroid/velocity merging, randomized neighbor order kills grid artifacts.

    DemoVideoJFA paperSPH paper
  • Radiance cascades 2D GI

    ~50 passes. 6 cascades (probe pitch 2→64px, rays 4→4096), premultiplied near/far merging, 4-tap bilinear parent fix-up kills halos, ping-pong cascade storage, f16 + RGB9E5 packing, analytic sky/sun top cascade. Fluid is grid-based semi-Lagrangian (advect→density→pressure→XSPH, 4 substeps).

    DemoVideoPaper
  • Temporal radiance cascades

    ~3.6k lines. World-space non-moving probes (no cascade re-fit on camera move), frame-rate-independent EMA accumulation with R2 probe jitter, cascade ping-pong (2 of 6 resident), RGB9E5 packing. Cellular-automata multi-biome map generation plus JFA fields and SPH liquid.

    DemoVideoPaper
  • 3D sandbox

    ~12k lines, 32 compute passes. CA-grown 2D map extruded to a 3D-box SDF, sphere-traced; VSM cone shadows (mip moments, Chebyshev bleed reduction); radiance cascades + 16×16 octahedral probes for indirect; log-luma histogram auto-exposure, HDR bloom, AgX, TAA in YCoCg with variance clipping.
